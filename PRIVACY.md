# Privacy

**In short: PDFCraft doesn't collect, send or share any of your data.**

## Your documents stay on your phone

Everything PDFCraft does happens on your device. That includes reading, editing, text recognition, conversion and encryption. There's no account to create and no cloud service behind the app.

The release version of the app doesn't request internet access, so Android won't let it connect to the internet at all.

## What the app stores

PDFCraft keeps these on your device only:

- **Your documents**, in the app's own storage, plus copies you choose to save to Downloads
- **Your library**: recent files, starred files, bookmarks and reading positions
- **Your settings**, such as theme and reading preferences
- **Saved signatures**, if you create any

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of it. Copies you saved to Downloads stay until you delete them.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it's used for |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Scanning documents. Requested only when you open the scanner. |
| Storage (Android 12 and older) | Finding PDFs on your phone and saving copies. |
| All files access (optional) | Listing PDFs anywhere on your device. Only turned on if you choose to. |

PDFCraft doesn't use analytics, ads, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

## Read aloud and text recognition

- **Read aloud** uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. Which voices you have installed, and how they work, depends on your device's settings.
- **Text recognition** uses Google's ML Kit with the recognition model bundled inside the app, so your scans are processed on the device.
